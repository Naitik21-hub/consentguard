# MeetLoop Privacy and Permissions Notice

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT: MeetLoop is a fictional video-calling app invented for ConsentGuard demos. This is not a real policy.

## What we collect and why

Account details: your name and mobile number, used to create your account and let contacts find you.

Microphone and camera: MeetLoop asks for microphone and camera access only when you start or join a call, so that others on the call can hear and see you. We do not record calls unless you press the Record button, and recordings are stored on your device.

Call metadata: the time, duration and participants of each call, used to show your call history and to detect abuse.

## Optional marketing messages

With your separate permission, we may send you offers and product news by email or SMS. This choice is off unless you switch it on, and saying no does not affect your ability to make calls.

## Sharing

We use a cloud hosting provider to run our service. It processes data only on our instructions. We do not sell your personal data.

## Retention

Call metadata is kept for 90 days and then deleted. Account details are deleted within 30 days after you delete your account.

## Withdrawing consent and your choices

You can turn off camera or microphone access at any time in your phone settings, and turn off marketing messages under Settings > Notifications > Marketing. Turning off marketing does not affect calls.
