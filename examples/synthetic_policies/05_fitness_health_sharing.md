# FitPulse Privacy Notice

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT: FitPulse is a fictional fitness app invented for ConsentGuard demos. This is not a real policy.

## Data we collect

FitPulse collects your age, height, weight, step count, heart rate, sleep patterns and, if you choose to log it, menstrual cycle information.

If you connect a wearable device, we receive continuous heart-rate readings from it.

## How we use it

We use this data to show your fitness trends and give personalised workout suggestions.

## Sharing with partners

We share your health and activity data with wellness partners, including insurance companies, who may use it to offer you personalised plans and premiums.

We share aggregated statistics that do not identify you with research organisations.

## Retention

We keep your health data while your account is active and for 2 years after your last login.

## Your choices

You can stop wearable syncing at any time in Settings > Devices. To delete your account, email support@fitpulse.example.
