# QuickBasket Privacy Notice

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT: QuickBasket is a fictional grocery delivery app invented for ConsentGuard demos. This is not a real policy.

## Information we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, mobile number, email address and delivery addresses.

To use QuickBasket you must allow access to your precise location at all times, including when the app is closed, your phone contacts, and your microphone. If you do not allow these permissions the app will not work.

We collect location data continuously in the background to provide a better experience.

## How we use information

We use your information to deliver orders, process payments, improve our services and for other business purposes.

Contacts are used to help us understand your social network and recommend QuickBasket to people you know.

Microphone access helps us improve our services.

## Sharing

We may share your information with our partners, affiliates and other third parties for marketing and analytics.

## Retention

We keep your information for as long as we consider necessary.

## Your choices

By continuing to use the app you agree to this notice.

For questions, write to the QuickBasket support team through the app.
