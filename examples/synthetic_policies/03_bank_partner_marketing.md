# Sahyog Digital Bank: Customer Privacy Notice

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT: Sahyog Digital Bank is a fictional bank invented for ConsentGuard demos. This is not a real policy.

## Information needed to open and run your account

To open and operate your savings account we collect your name, date of birth, address, PAN, photograph and identity documents. This information is required to complete Know Your Customer (KYC) checks required by law and banking regulations, and to provide the account services you request.

We process your transaction records to run your account, prevent fraud and meet our legal reporting duties.

## Marketing and partners

We may also use your information, including your transaction patterns, to offer you products from Sahyog and our select partners, and may share relevant information with such partners to enable them to contact you with suitable offers.

## Retention

We retain account and KYC records for the periods required by applicable law after your account is closed.

## Your choices

You can contact our Grievance Redressal Officer at grievance@sahyogbank.example for any privacy concern.
