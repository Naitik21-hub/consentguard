# Kitaab Library App: Privacy Notice

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT: Kitaab is a fictional public-library app invented for ConsentGuard demos. This is not a real policy.

## What we collect

We collect only your name, library card number and email address, to let you borrow e-books and receive due-date reminders.

We do not request location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

## How we use it

Your borrowing history is used only to show your current loans and send due-date reminders. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## Optional reading recommendations

If you switch on "Recommendations" in Settings, we use your borrowing history to suggest books. This is off by default and you can switch it off at any time; borrowing works the same either way.

## Sharing

We share your email address with our email delivery provider only to send reminders. We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

## Retention

Loan records are deleted 30 days after a book is returned. If you close your account, your remaining data is deleted within 30 days.

## Withdrawal and contact

You can withdraw consent for recommendations in Settings > Recommendations, as easily as you turned it on. For any privacy question or complaint, contact our Grievance Officer at grievance@kitaab.example. We reply within 7 working days.
